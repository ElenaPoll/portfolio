About me

I am a designer with a background evolving from product and communication design to the digital sphere and experience design. My profile combines a solid academic root — culminating in a PhD in Design dedicated to accessibility and inclusiveness in digital ecosystems — to a strongly strategic vision.

I integrate research, strategy, and design to translate complex challenges into structured solutions, accompanying initiatives from concept to delivery, with a method in which creative vision is constantly fused with organizational rigor and coordination.

Areas of intervention:
  • Digital & Inclusive Experience Design: design of accessible, conscious, and person-centered user experiences.
  • Project Management & Coordination: end-to-end project management and development, from initial brief to realization.
  • Strategic Consulting: translation of systemic complexities into clear operative strategies.
