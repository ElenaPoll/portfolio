About me

I am a designer with a background evolving from product and communication design to the digital sphere and experience design. My profile combines a solid academic root — culminating in a PhD in Design dedicated to accessibility and inclusiveness in digital ecosystems — to a strongly strategic vision.

I integrate research, strategy, and design to translate complex challenges into structured solutions, accompanying initiatives from concept to delivery, with a method in which creative vision is constantly fused with organizational rigor and coordination.

Areas of intervention:
  • Digital & Inclusive Experience Design: design of accessible, conscious, and person-centered user experiences.
  • Project Management & Coordination: end-to-end project management and development, from initial brief to realization.
  • Strategic Consulting: translation of systemic complexities into clear operative strategies.

Designing for compliance

USER RESEARCH - SEMANTIC ARCHITECTURE - WCAG COMPLIANCE & AUDITING - EMPIRICAL VALIDATION - SEMANTIC HTML & INCLUSIVE CSS - PROJECT COORDINATION

I translated complex WCAG compliance mandates into an intuitive interface architecture that addresses core business and regulatory requirements. Through both bottom-up and top-down analysis, I combined detailed assessment of accessibility barriers, technical constraints, and interaction patterns with a broader understanding of user needs, organizational objectives, and compliance strategy. Grounded in user research, I designed and validated a fully compliant digital experience, translating complex accessibility guidelines into clear, actionable design solutions. I built a robust semantic HTML and CSS foundation and conducted rigorous accessibility testing across navigation, interaction, content structure, and user flows, ensuring a seamless and inclusive experience for all users.

Designing for inclusion

PARTNER & ASSOCIATION COORDINATION - SERVICE DESIGN - PARTICIPATORY DESIGN WORKSHOP LEADERSHIP - CROSS-FUNCTIONAL STAKEHOLDER ALIGNMENT - DIGITAL EXPERIENCE DESIGN

I led user research, co-design strategy, and digital prototyping for a Living Lab initiative funded by the Municipality of Genoa to enhance domestic autonomy for visually impaired seniors. Translating field research into digital tools, I conducted immersive simulations with therapists and 30+ participants to map accessibility barriers, then architected a mobile-ready evaluation framework. This interactive protocol empowered field specialists to optimize home environments, bridging academic researchers, healthcare foundations, and municipal stakeholders around a unified solution.
